# 1 Chronicles 8

### 1
Benjamin became the father of Bela his firstborn, Ashbel the second, Aharah the third, Nohah the fourth, and Rapha the fifth.

### 2
Bela had sons: Addar, Gera, Abihud, Abishua, Naaman, Ahoah,

### 3
Gera, Shephuphan, and Huram.

### 4
These are the sons of Ehud. These are the heads of fathers’ households of the inhabitants of Geba, who were carried captive to Manahath: Naaman, Ahijah, and Gera, who carried them captive; and he became the father of Uzza and Ahihud.

### 5
Shaharaim became the father of children in the field of Moab, after he had sent them away. Hushim and Baara were his wives.

### 6
By Hodesh his wife, he became the father of Jobab, Zibia, Mesha, Malcam, Jeuz, Shachia, and Mirmah. These were his sons, heads of fathers’ households.

### 7
By Hushim, he became the father of Abitub and Elpaal.

### 8
The sons of Elpaal: Eber, Misham, and Shemed, who built Ono and Lod, with its towns; and Beriah, and Shema, who were heads of fathers’ households of the inhabitants of Aijalon, who put to flight the inhabitants of Gath;

### 9
and Ahio, Shashak, Jeremoth, Zebadiah, Arad, Eder,

### 10
Michael, Ishpah, Joha, the sons of Beriah, Zebadiah, Meshullam, Hizki, Heber,

### 11
Ishmerai, Izliah, Jobab, the sons of Elpaal, Jakim, Zichri, Zabdi,

### 12
Elienai, Zillethai, Eliel, Adaiah, Beraiah, Shimrath, the sons of Shimei,

### 13
Ishpan, Eber, Eliel, Abdon, Zichri, Hanan,

### 14
Hananiah, Elam, Anthothijah, Iphdeiah, Penuel, the sons of Shashak,

### 15
Shamsherai, Shehariah, Athaliah, Jaareshiah, Elijah, Zichri, and the sons of Jeroham.

### 16
These were heads of fathers’ households throughout their generations, chief men. These lived in Jerusalem.

### 17
The father of Gibeon, whose wife’s name was Maacah, lived in Gibeon, with his firstborn son Abdon, Zur, Kish, Baal, Nadab,

### 18
Gedor, Ahio, and Zecher.

### 19
Mikloth became the father of Shimeah. They also lived with their brothers in Jerusalem, near their brothers.

### 20
Ner became the father of Kish. Kish became the father of Saul. Saul became the father of Jonathan, Malchishua, Abinadab, and Eshbaal.

### 21
The son of Jonathan was Merib Baal. Merib Baal became the father of Micah.

### 22
The sons of Micah: Pithon, Melech, Tarea, and Ahaz.

### 23
Ahaz became the father of Jehoaddah. Jehoaddah became the father of Alemeth, Azmaveth, and Zimri. Zimri became the father of Moza.

### 24
Moza became the father of Binea. Raphah was his son, Eleasah his son, and Azel his son.

### 25
Azel had six sons, whose names are these: Azrikam, Bocheru, Ishmael, Sheariah, Obadiah, and Hanan. All these were the sons of Azel.

### 26
The sons of Eshek his brother: Ulam his firstborn, Jeush the second, and Eliphelet the third.

### 27
The sons of Ulam were mighty men of valor, archers, and had many sons, and sons’ sons, one hundred fifty. All these were of the sons of Benjamin.
