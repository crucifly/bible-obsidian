# 1 Meqabyan 1

### 1
In the days of Meqabees (Maccabees), there was a man whose name was Akrandis, King of Media and Chaldea. He was a great and powerful man; he was wealthy and had much gold and silver.

### 2
He built a city and called it after his own name, Akrandis. He also made an image of an idol and called it after his own name, Akrandis.

### 3
He commanded all the people to bow down to that image and to worship it. If anyone did not bow down to it, they would be killed by the sword and burned in the fire.

### 4
At that time, three brothers from the children of Israel were found there: Meqabyan (Maccabees), Abya, and Panos. They were the sons of a man named Meqabyan, of the tribe of Benjamin.

### 5
They refused to bow down to the image of the idol, saying: "We will not bow down to an idol made by hands, but we will worship the Lord our God, the Creator of Heaven and Earth."

### 6
When the King heard this, he was provoked to anger and commanded that they be brought before him.

### 7
When they stood before the King, he said to them: "Why do you not bow down to my image and worship it? Do you not know that I have the power to kill you or to save you?"

### 8
They answered and said to the King: "O King, we do not fear your fire or your sword. Our God, whom we serve, is able to save us from your hand; but even if He does not, we will not bow down to your image."
