# 1 Meqabyan 4

### 1
Meqabyan and his brothers fled to the mountains and gathered an army of the children of Israel.

### 2
They fought against the King’s army and defeated them. They killed many of the Syrians and the Edomites.

### 3
Meqabyan was a brave man; he fought for the Law of the Lord and for the honored city, Jerusalem.

### 4
He defeated the King’s generals and captured their wealth.

### 5
The King was provoked to anger and gathered a great army from all his regions to destroy Meqabyan and the children of Israel.
