# 1 Meqabyan 7

### 1
After the death of Meqabyan, his brothers continued to fight for the Law of the Lord.

### 2
They defeated their enemies and saved the children of Israel.

### 3
They ruled in Jerusalem and kept the Command of the Lord.

### 4
This is the book of 1 Meqabyan, which tells of the struggle of the children of Israel for the Law of their God.
