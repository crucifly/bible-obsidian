# 1 Meqabyan 6

### 1
But the King did not stop his evil works. He sent another army against Meqabyan.

### 2
Meqabyan fought against them and was killed in the battle.

### 3
His brothers, Abya and Panos, buried him in the city of their fathers.

### 4
All the children of Israel wept for him, saying: "A great man has fallen in Israel today."
