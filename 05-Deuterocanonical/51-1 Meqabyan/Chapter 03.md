# 1 Meqabyan 3

### 1
After this, there arose another King whose name was Tseerutsaydan (Antiochus). He was a wicked and sinful man.

### 2
He came to Jerusalem and destroyed the Temple of the Lord. He killed many of the children of Israel and took their children and their wealth as plunder.

### 3
He commanded that the children of Israel should not keep the Law of Moses, nor should they circumcise their children.

### 4
He also commanded that they should eat the flesh of swine and sacrifice to idols.

### 5
Many of the children of Israel were afraid and did as the King commanded; but Meqabyan and his brothers remained firm in the Law of the Lord.
