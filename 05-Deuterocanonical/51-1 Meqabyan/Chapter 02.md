# 1 Meqabyan 2

### 1
At that time, the King was filled with wrath and commanded that a fire be lit in a furnace, seven times hotter than usual.

### 2
He commanded that the three brothers be bound and cast into the midst of the furnace.

### 3
But the Lord sent His Angel, who cooled the fire for them, so that they were not burned. They stood in the midst of the fire and praised the Lord.

### 4
When the King saw this, he was astonished and said: "Did we not cast three men into the fire? Behold, I see four men walking in the midst of the fire, and they are not burned; and the appearance of the fourth is like the Son of God."

### 5
Then the King commanded them to come out of the fire. When they came out, their clothes were not burned, nor was there any smell of fire upon them.

### 6
Then the King believed in the Lord and commanded that all the people worship the God of Meqabyan, Abya, and Panos.
