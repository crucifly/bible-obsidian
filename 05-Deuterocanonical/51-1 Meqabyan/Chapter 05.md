# 1 Meqabyan 5

### 1
Meqabyan prayed to the Lord, saying: "O Lord, God of our fathers, save us from the hand of this wicked King. For You are our help and our strength."

### 2
The Lord heard his prayer and gave him victory over the King’s army.

### 3
Meqabyan killed the King’s generals and destroyed his army. He took much plunder and returned to Jerusalem with joy.

### 4
He rebuilt the Temple and restored the Law of the Lord.
