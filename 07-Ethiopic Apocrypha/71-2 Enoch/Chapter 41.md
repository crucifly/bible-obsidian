# Chapter 41

### 1
And I saw all forefathers from (all) time withAdam and Eva (Eve)(http://scriptures.lds.org/bde/eve), and I sighed and broke into tears and said of the ruin of their dishonour: Woe is me for my infirmity and (for that) of my forefathers, and thought in my heart and said:

### 2
Blessed (is) the man who has not been born or who has been born and shall not sin before the Lords face, that he come not into this place, nor bring the yoke of this place.
