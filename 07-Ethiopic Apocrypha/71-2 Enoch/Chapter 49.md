# Chapter 49

### 49-1
I swear to you, my children, but I swear not by any oath, neither byheaven nor by earth, nor by any other creature which God created.
### 49-2
The Lord said: There is no oath in me, nor injustice, but truth.
### 49-3
If there is no truth in men, let them swear by the words, Yea, yea, or else, Nay, nay.
### 49-4
And I swear to you, yea, yea, that there has been no man in his mothers womb, (but that) already before, even to each one there is a place prepared for the repose of that soul, and a measure fixed how much it is intended that a man be tried in this world.
### 49-5
Yea, children, deceive not yourselves, for there has been previously prepared a place for every soul of man.
