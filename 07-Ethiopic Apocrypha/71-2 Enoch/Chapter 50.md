# Chapter 50

### 50-1
I have put every mans work in writing and none born on earth can remain hidden nor his works remain concealed.

### 50-2
I see all things.

### 50-3
Now therefore, my children, in patience and meekness spend the number of your days, that you inherit endless life.

### 50-4
Endure for the sake of theLord every wound, every injury, every evil word and attack.

### 50-5
If ill-requitals befall you, return (them) not either to neighbour or enemy, because the Lord will return (them) for you and be your avenger on the day of great judgment, that there be no avenging here among men.

### 50-6
Whoever of you spends gold or silver for his brothers sake, he will receive ample treasure in the world to come.

### 50-7
Injure not widows nor orphans nor strangers, lest Gods wrath come upon you.
