# Chapter 11

### 1
### 11-1

### 2
Blow ye in Zion on the trumpet to summon (the) saints,

### 3
### 11-2

### 4
Cause ye to be heard in Jerusalem the voice of him that bringeth good tidings; For God hath had pity on Israel in visiting them.

### 5
### 11-3

### 6
Stand on the height, O Jerusalem, and behold thy children, From the East and the West, gathered together by the Lord;

### 7
### 11-4

### 8
From the North they come in the gladness of their God, From the isles afar off God hath gathered them.

### 9
### 11-5

### 10
High mountains hath He abased into a plain for them;

### 11
### 11-6

### 12
The hills fled at their entrance. The woods gave them shelter as they passed by;

### 13
### 11-7

### 14
Every sweet-smelling tree God caused to spring up for them, That Israel might pass by in the visitation of the glory of their God.

### 15
### 11-8

### 16
Put on, O Jerusalem, thy glorious garments; Make ready thy holy robe; For God hath spoken good concerning Israel, for ever and ever.

### 17
### 11-9

### 18
Let the Lord do what He hath spoken concerning Israel and Jerusalem; Let the Lord raise up Israel by His glorious name. The mercy of the Lord be upon Israel for ever and ever.
