# Chapter 8

### 1
### 8-1

### 2
Distress and the sound of war hath my ear heard; The sound of a trumpet announcing slaughter and calamity,

### 3
### 8-2

### 4
The sound of much people as of an exceeding high wind, As a tempest with mighty fire sweeping through the Negeb.

### 5
### 8-3

### 6
And I said in my heart; Surely () God judgeth us;

### 7
### 8-4

### 8
A sound I hear (moving) towards Jerusalem, the holy city.

### 9
### 8-5

### 10
My loins were broken at what I heard, my knees tottered:

### 11
### 8-6

### 12
My heart was afraid, my bones were dismayed like flax.

### 13
### 8-7

### 14
I said: They establish their ways in righteousness. I thought upon the judgments of God since the creation of heaven and earth; I held God righteous in His judgments which have been from of old.

### 15
### 8-8

### 16
God laid bare their sins in the full light of day; All the earth came to know the righteous judgments of God.

### 17
### 8-9

### 18
In secret places underground their iniquities (were committed) to provoke (Him) to anger;

### 19
### 8-10

### 20
They wrought confusion, son with mother and father with daughter;

### 21
### 8-11

### 22
They committed adultery, every man with his neighbor's wife. They concluded covenants with one another with an oath touching these things;

### 23
### 8-12

### 24
They plundered the sanctuary of God, as though there was no avenger.

### 25
### 8-13

### 26
They trode the altar of the Lord, (coming straight) from all manner of uncleanness; And with menstrual blood they defiled the sacrifices, as (though these were) common flesh.

### 27
### 8-14

### 28
They left no sin undone, wherein they surpassed not the heathen.

### 29
### 8-15

### 30
Therefore God mingled for them a spirit of wandering; And gave them to drink a cup of undiluted wine, that they might become drunken.

### 31
### 8-16

### 32
He brought him that is from the end of the earth, that smiteth mightily;

### 33
### 8-17

### 34
He decreed () war against Jerusalem, and against her land.

### 35
### 8-18

### 36
The princes of the land went to meet him with joy: they said unto him: Blessed be thy way! Come ye, enter ye in with peace.

### 37
### 8-19

### 38
They made the rough ways even, before his entering in; They opened the gates to Jerusalem, they crowned its walls.

### 39
### 8-20

### 40
As a father (entereth) the house of his sons, (so) he entered (Jerusalem) in peace; He established his feet (there) in great safety.

### 41
### 8-21

### 42
He captured her fortresses and the wall of Jerusalem;

### 43
### 8-22

### 44
For God Himself led him in safety, while they wandered.

### 45
### 8-23

### 46
He destroyed their princes and every one wise in counsel; He poured out the blood of the inhabitants of Jerusalem, like the water of uncleanness.

### 47
### 8-24

### 48
He led away their sons and daughters, whom they had begotten in defilement.

### 49
### 8-25

### 50
They did according to their uncleanness, even as their fathers (had done):

### 51
### 8-26

### 52
They defiled Jerusalem and the things that had been hallowed to the name of God.

### 53
### 8-27

### 54
(But) God hath shown Himself righteous in His judgments upon the nations of the earth;

### 55
### 8-28

### 56
And the pious (servants) of God are like innocent lambs in their midst.

### 57
### 8-29

### 58
Worthy to be praised is the Lord that judgeth the whole earth in His righteousness.

### 59
### 8-30

### 60
Behold, now, O God, Thou hast shown us Thy judgment in Thy righteousness;

### 61
### 8-31

### 62
Our eyes have seen Thy judgments, O God. We have justified Thy name that is honoured for ever;

### 63
### 8-32

### 64
For Thou art the God of righteousness, judging Israel with chastening.

### 65
### 8-33

### 66
Turn, O God, Thy mercy upon us, and have pity upon us;

### 67
### 8-34

### 68
Gather together the dispersed of Israel, with mercy and goodness;

### 69
### 8-35

### 70
For Thy faithfulness is with us. And (though) we have stiffened our neck, yet Thou art our chastener;

### 71
### 8-36

### 72
Overlook us not, O our God, lest the nations swallow us up, as though there were none to deliver.

### 73
### 8-37

### 74
But Thou art our God from the beginning, And upon Thee is our hope (set), O Lord;

### 75
### 8-38

### 76
And we will not depart from Thee, For good are Thy judgments upon us.

### 77
### 8-39

### 78
Ours and our children's be Thy good pleasure for ever; O Lord our Saviour, we shall never more be moved.

### 79
### 8-40

### 80
The Lord is worthy to be praised for His judgments with the mouth of His pious ones; And blessed be Israel of the Lord for ever.
