# Chapter 8

### 1
Distress and the sound of war hath my ear heard; The sound of a trumpet announcing slaughter and calamity, The sound of much people as of an exceeding high wind, As a tempest with mighty fire sweeping through the Negeb.

### 2
And I said in my heart; Surely () God judgeth us; A sound I hear (moving) towards Jerusalem, the holy city.

### 3
My loins were broken at what I heard, my knees tottered: My heart was afraid, my bones were dismayed like flax.

### 4
I said: They establish their ways in righteousness. I thought upon the judgments of God since the creation of heaven and earth; I held God righteous in His judgments which have been from of old.

### 5
God laid bare their sins in the full light of day; All the earth came to know the righteous judgments of God.

### 6
In secret places underground their iniquities (were committed) to provoke (Him) to anger; They wrought confusion, son with mother and father with daughter;

### 7
They committed adultery, every man with his neighbor's wife. They concluded covenants with one another with an oath touching these things; They plundered the sanctuary of God, as though there was no avenger.

### 8
They trode the altar of the Lord, (coming straight) from all manner of uncleanness; And with menstrual blood they defiled the sacrifices, as (though these were) common flesh.

### 9
They left no sin undone, wherein they surpassed not the heathen.

### 10
Therefore God mingled for them a spirit of wandering; And gave them to drink a cup of undiluted wine, that they might become drunken.

### 11
He brought him that is from the end of the earth, that smiteth mightily; He decreed () war against Jerusalem, and against her land.

### 12
The princes of the land went to meet him with joy: they said unto him: Blessed be thy way! Come ye, enter ye in with peace.

### 13
They made the rough ways even, before his entering in; They opened the gates to Jerusalem, they crowned its walls.

### 14
As a father (entereth) the house of his sons, (so) he entered (Jerusalem) in peace; He established his feet (there) in great safety.

### 15
He captured her fortresses and the wall of Jerusalem; For God Himself led him in safety, while they wandered.

### 16
He destroyed their princes and every one wise in counsel; He poured out the blood of the inhabitants of Jerusalem, like the water of uncleanness.

### 17
He led away their sons and daughters, whom they had begotten in defilement.

### 18
They did according to their uncleanness, even as their fathers (had done): They defiled Jerusalem and the things that had been hallowed to the name of God.

### 19
(But) God hath shown Himself righteous in His judgments upon the nations of the earth; And the pious (servants) of God are like innocent lambs in their midst.

### 20
Worthy to be praised is the Lord that judgeth the whole earth in His righteousness.

### 21
Behold, now, O God, Thou hast shown us Thy judgment in Thy righteousness; Our eyes have seen Thy judgments, O God. We have justified Thy name that is honoured for ever;

### 22
For Thou art the God of righteousness, judging Israel with chastening.

### 23
Turn, O God, Thy mercy upon us, and have pity upon us; Gather together the dispersed of Israel, with mercy and goodness;

### 24
For Thy faithfulness is with us. And (though) we have stiffened our neck, yet Thou art our chastener; Overlook us not, O our God, lest the nations swallow us up, as though there were none to deliver.

### 25
But Thou art our God from the beginning, And upon Thee is our hope (set), O Lord; And we will not depart from Thee, For good are Thy judgments upon us.

### 26
Ours and our children's be Thy good pleasure for ever; O Lord our Saviour, we shall never more be moved.

### 27
The Lord is worthy to be praised for His judgments with the mouth of His pious ones; And blessed be Israel of the Lord for ever.
