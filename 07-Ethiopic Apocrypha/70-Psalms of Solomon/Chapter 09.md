# Chapter 9

### 1
### 9-1

### 2
When Israel was led away captive into a strange land, When they fell away from the Lord who redeemed them,

### 3
### 9-2

### 4
They were cast away from the inheritance, which Lord had given them. Among every nation (were) the dispersed of Israel according to the word of God,

### 5
### 9-3

### 6
That Thou mightest be justified, O God, in Thy righteousness by reason of our transgressions:

### 7
### 9-4

### 8
For Thou art a just judge over all the peoples of the earth.

### 9
### 9-5

### 10
For from Thy knowledge none that doeth unjustly is hidden,

### 11
### 9-6

### 12
And the righteous deeds of Thy pious ones (are) before Thee, O Lord, Where, then, can a man hide himself from Thy knowledge, O God

### 13
### 9-7

### 14
Our works are subject to our own choice and power To do right or wrong in the works of our hands

### 15
### 9-8

### 16
And in Thy righteousness Thou visitest the sons of men.

### 17
### 9-9

### 18
He that doeth righteousness layeth up life for himself with the Lord; And he that doeth wrongly forfeits his life to destruction;

### 19
### 9-10

### 20
For the judgments of the Lord are (given) in righteousness to (every) man and (his) house. Unto whom art Thou good, O God, except to them that call upon the Lord

### 21
### 9-12

### 22
He cleanseth from sins a soul when it maketh confession, when it maketh acknowledgement;

### 23
### 9-13

### 24
For shame is upon us and upon our faces on account of all these things.

### 25
### 9-14

### 26
And to whom doth He forgive sins, except to them that have sinned

### 27
### 9-15

### 28
Thou blessest the righteous, and dost not reprove them for the sins that they have committed; And Thy goodness is upon them that sin, when they repent.

### 29
### 9-16

### 30
And, now, Thou art our God, and we the people whom Thou hast loved: Behold and show pity, O God of Israel, for we are Thine; And remove not Thy mercy from us, lest they assail us.

### 31
### 9-17

### 32
For Thou didst choose the seed of Abraham before all the nations, And didst set Thy name upon us, O Lord,

### 33
### 9-18

### 34
And Thou wilt not reject (us) for ever. Thou madest a covenant with our fathers concerning us;

### 35
### 9-19

### 36
And we hope in Thee, when our soul turneth (unto Thee). The mercy of the Lord be upon the house of Israel for ever and ever.
