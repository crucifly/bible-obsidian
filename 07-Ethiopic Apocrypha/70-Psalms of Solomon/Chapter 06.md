# Chapter 6

### 1
Happy is the man whose heart is fixed to call upon the name of the Lord; When he remembereth the name of the Lord, he will be saved.

### 2
His ways are made even by the Lord, And the works of his hands are preserved by the Lord his God.

### 3
At what he sees in his bad dreams, his soul shall not be troubled; When he passes through rivers and the tossing of the seas, he shall not be dismayed.

### 4
He ariseth from his sleep, and blesseth the name of the Lord: When his heart is at peace, he singeth to the name of his God, And he entreateth the Lord for all his house.

### 5
And the Lord heareth the prayer of every one that feareth God, And every request of the soul that hopes for Him doth the Lord accomplish.

### 6
Blessed is the Lord, who showeth mercy to those who love Him in sincerity.
