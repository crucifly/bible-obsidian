# Chapter 15

### 1
### 15-1

### 2
When I was in distress I called upon the name of the Lord, I hoped for the help of the God of Jacob and was saved;

### 3
### 15-2

### 4
For the hope and refuge of the poor art Thou, O God.

### 5
### 15-3

### 6
(a) For who, O God, is strong except to give thanks unto Thee in truth

### 7
### 15-4

### 8
And wherein is a man powerful except in giving thanks to Thy name

### 9
### 15-5

### 10
A new psalm with song in gladness of heart,

### 11
### 15-6

### 12
He that offereth these things shall never be shaken by evil; The flame of fire and the wrath against the unrighteous shall not touch him,

### 13
### 15-7

### 14
When it goeth forth from the face of the Lord against sinners, To destroy all the substance of sinners,

### 15
### 15-8

### 16
For the mark of God is upon the righteous that they may be saved. Famine and sword and pestilence (shall be) far from the righteous,

### 17
### 15-9

### 18
For they shall flee away from the pious as men pursued in war; But they shall pursue sinners and overtake (them), And they that do lawlessness shall not escape the judgment of God; As by enemies experienced (in war) shall they be overtaken,

### 19
### 15-10

### 20
For the mark of destruction is upon their forehead.

### 21
### 15-11

### 22
And the inheritance of sinners is destruction and darkness, And their iniquities shall pursue them unto Sheol beneath.

### 23
### 15-12

### 24
Their inheritance shall not be found of their children,

### 25
### 15-13

### 26
For sins shall lay waste the houses of sinners. And sinners shall perish for ever in the day of the Lord's judgment,

### 27
### 15-14

### 28
When God visiteth the earth with His judgment.

### 29
### 15-15

### 30
But they that fear the Lord shall find mercy therein, And shall live by the compassion of their God; But sinners shall perish for ever.
