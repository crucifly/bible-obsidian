# Job 12

### 1
Then Job answered, “No doubt, but you are the people, and wisdom shall die with you.

### 2
But I have understanding as well as you; I am not inferior to you. Yes, who doesn’t know such things as these?

### 3
I am like one who is a joke to his neighbor, I, who called on God, and he answered. The just, the blameless man is a joke.

### 4
In the thought of him who is at ease there is contempt for misfortune. It is ready for them whose foot slips.

### 5
The tents of robbers prosper. Those who provoke God are secure, who carry their God in their hands.

### 6
“But ask the animals, now, and they shall teach you; the birds of the sky, and they shall tell you.

### 7
Or speak to the earth, and it shall teach you. The fish of the sea shall declare to you.

### 8
Who doesn’t know that in all these, Yahweh’s hand has done this, in whose hand is the life of every living thing, and the breath of all mankind?

### 9
Doesn’t the ear try words, even as the palate tastes its food?

### 10
With aged men is wisdom, in length of days understanding.

### 11
“With God is wisdom and might. He has counsel and understanding.

### 12
Behold, he breaks down, and it can’t be built again. He imprisons a man, and there can be no release.

### 13
Behold, he withholds the waters, and they dry up. Again, he sends them out, and they overturn the earth.

### 14
With him is strength and wisdom. The deceived and the deceiver are his.

### 15
He leads counselors away stripped. He makes judges fools.

### 16
He loosens the bond of kings. He binds their waist with a belt.

### 17
He leads priests away stripped, and overthrows the mighty.

### 18
He removes the speech of those who are trusted, and takes away the understanding of the elders.

### 19
He pours contempt on princes, and loosens the belt of the strong.

### 20
He uncovers deep things out of darkness, and brings out to light the shadow of death.

### 21
He increases the nations, and he destroys them. He enlarges the nations, and he leads them captive.

### 22
He takes away understanding from the chiefs of the people of the earth, and causes them to wander in a wilderness where there is no way.

### 23
They grope in the dark without light. He makes them stagger like a drunken man.
