# Job 15

### 1
Then Eliphaz the Temanite answered, “Should a wise man answer with vain knowledge, and fill himself with the east wind?

### 2
Should he reason with unprofitable talk, or with speeches with which he can do no good?

### 3
Yes, you do away with fear, and hinder devotion before God.

### 4
For your iniquity teaches your mouth, and you choose the language of the crafty.

### 5
Your own mouth condemns you, and not I. Yes, your own lips testify against you.

### 6
“Are you the first man who was born? Or were you brought out before the hills?

### 7
Have you heard the secret counsel of God? Do you limit wisdom to yourself?

### 8
What do you know, that we don’t know? What do you understand, which is not in us?

### 9
With us are both the gray-headed and the very aged men, much elder than your father.

### 10
Are the consolations of God too small for you, even the word that is gentle toward you?

### 11
Why does your heart carry you away? Why do your eyes flash, That you turn your spirit against God, and let such words go out of your mouth?

### 12
What is man, that he should be clean? What is he who is born of a woman, that he should be righteous?

### 13
Behold, he puts no trust in his holy ones. Yes, the heavens are not clean in his sight; how much less one who is abominable and corrupt, a man who drinks iniquity like water!

### 14
“I will show you, listen to me; that which I have seen I will declare (which wise men have told by their fathers, and have not hidden it;

### 15
to whom alone the land was given, and no stranger passed among them): the wicked man writhes in pain all his days, even the number of years that are laid up for the oppressor.

### 16
A sound of terrors is in his ears. In prosperity the destroyer shall come on him.

### 17
He doesn’t believe that he shall return out of darkness. He is waited for by the sword.

### 18
He wanders abroad for bread, saying, ‘Where is it?’ He knows that the day of darkness is ready at his hand.

### 19
Distress and anguish make him afraid. They prevail against him, as a king ready to the battle.

### 20
Because he has stretched out his hand against God, and behaves himself proudly against the Almighty; he runs at him with a stiff neck, with the thick shields of his bucklers;

### 21
because he has covered his face with his fatness, and gathered fat on his thighs.

### 22
He has lived in desolate cities, in houses which no one inhabited, which were ready to become heaps.

### 23
He shall not be rich, neither shall his substance continue, neither shall their possessions be extended on the earth.

### 24
He shall not depart out of darkness. The flame shall dry up his branches. By the breath of God’s mouth shall he go away.

### 25
Let him not trust in emptiness, deceiving himself; for emptiness shall be his reward.

### 26
It shall be accomplished before his time. His branch shall not be green.

### 27
He shall shake off his unripe grape as the vine, and shall cast off his flower as the olive tree.

### 28
For the company of the godless shall be barren, and fire shall consume the tents of bribery.

### 29
They conceive mischief, and produce iniquity. Their heart prepares deceit.”
