# Job 27

### 1
Job again took up his parable, and said, “As God lives, who has taken away my right, the Almighty, who has made my soul bitter

### 2
(for the length of my life is still in me, and the spirit of God is in my nostrils); surely my lips shall not speak unrighteousness, neither shall my tongue utter deceit.

### 3
Far be it from me that I should justify you. Until I die I will not put away my integrity from me.

### 4
I hold fast to my righteousness, and will not let it go. My heart shall not reproach me so long as I live.

### 5
“Let my enemy be as the wicked. Let him who rises up against me be as the unrighteous.

### 6
For what is the hope of the godless, when he is cut off, when God takes away his life?

### 7
Will God hear his cry when trouble comes on him?

### 8
Will he delight himself in the Almighty, and call on God at all times?

### 9
I will teach you about the hand of God. That which is with the Almighty will I not conceal.

### 10
Behold, all of you have seen it yourselves; why then have you become altogether vain?

### 11
“This is the portion of a wicked man with God, the heritage of oppressors, which they receive from the Almighty.

### 12
If his children are multiplied, it is for the sword. His offspring shall not be satisfied with bread.

### 13
Those who remain of him shall be buried in death. His widows shall make no lamentation.

### 14
Though he heap up silver as the dust, and prepare clothing as the clay; he may prepare it, but the just shall put it on, and the innocent shall divide the silver.

### 15
He builds his house as the moth, as a booth which the watchman makes.

### 16
He lies down rich, but he shall not do so again. He opens his eyes, and he is not.

### 17
Terrors overtake him like waters. A storm steals him away in the night.

### 18
The east wind carries him away, and he departs. It sweeps him out of his place.

### 19
For it hurls at him, and does not spare, as he flees away from his hand.

### 20
Men shall clap their hands at him, and shall hiss him out of his place.
