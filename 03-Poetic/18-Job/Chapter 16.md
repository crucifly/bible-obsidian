# Job 16

### 1
Then Job answered, “I have heard many such things. You are all miserable comforters!

### 2
Shall vain words have an end? Or what provokes you that you answer?

### 3
I also could speak as you do. If your soul were in my soul’s place, I could join words together against you, and shake my head at you, but I would strengthen you with my mouth. The solace of my lips would relieve you.

### 4
“Though I speak, my grief is not subsided. Though I forbear, what am I eased?

### 5
But now, God, you have surely worn me out. You have made desolate all my company.

### 6
You have shriveled me up. This is a witness against me. My leanness rises up against me. It testifies to my face.

### 7
He has torn me in his wrath, and persecuted me. He has gnashed on me with his teeth. My adversary sharpens his eyes on me.

### 8
They have gaped on me with their mouth. They have struck me on the cheek reproachfully. They gather themselves together against me.

### 9
God delivers me to the ungodly, and casts me into the hands of the wicked.

### 10
I was at ease, and he broke me apart. Yes, he has taken me by the neck, and dashed me to pieces. He has also set me up for his target.

### 11
His archers surround me. He splits my kidneys apart, and does not spare. He pours out my gall on the ground.

### 12
He breaks me with breach on breach. He runs on me like a giant.

### 13
I have sewed sackcloth on my skin, and have thrust my horn in the dust.

### 14
My face is red with weeping. Deep darkness is on my eyelids.

### 15
Although there is no violence in my hands, and my prayer is pure.

### 16
“Earth, don’t cover my blood. Let my cry have no place to rest.

### 17
Even now, behold, my witness is in heaven. He who vouches for me is on high.

### 18
My friends scoff at me. My eyes pour out tears to God, that he would maintain the right of a man with God, of a son of man with his neighbor!

### 19
For when a few years have come, I shall go the way of no return.
