# Proverbs 1

### 1
The proverbs of Solomon, the son of David, king of Israel: to know wisdom and instruction; to discern the words of understanding;

### 2
to receive instruction in wise dealing, in righteousness, justice, and equity; to give prudence to the simple, knowledge and discretion to the young man:

### 3
that the wise man may hear, and increase in learning; that the man of understanding may attain to sound counsel: to understand a proverb, and parables, the words and riddles of the wise.

### 4
The fear of Yahweh is the beginning of knowledge; but the foolish despise wisdom and instruction.

### 5
My son, listen to your father’s instruction, and don’t forsake your mother’s teaching: for they will be a garland to grace your head, and chains around your neck.

### 6
My son, if sinners entice you, don’t consent.

### 7
If they say, “Come with us. Let’s lay in wait for blood. Let’s lurk secretly for the innocent without cause.

### 8
Let’s swallow them up alive like Sheol, and whole, like those who go down into the pit.

### 9
We’ll find all valuable wealth. We’ll fill our houses with plunder.

### 10
You shall cast your lot among us. We’ll all have one purse.”

### 11
My son, don’t walk on the path with them. Keep your foot from their path, for their feet run to evil. They hurry to shed blood.

### 12
For in vain is the net spread in the sight of any bird: but these lay wait for their own blood. They lurk secretly for their own lives.

### 13
So are the ways of everyone who is greedy for gain. It takes away the life of its owners.

### 14
Wisdom calls aloud in the street. She utters her voice in the public squares.

### 15
She calls at the head of noisy places. At the entrance of the city gates, she utters her words: “How long, you simple ones, will you love simplicity? How long will mockers delight themselves in mockery, and fools hate knowledge?

### 16
Turn at my reproof. Behold, I will pour out my spirit on you. I will make known my words to you.

### 17
Because I have called, and you have refused; I have stretched out my hand, and no one has paid attention; but you have ignored all my counsel, and wanted none of my reproof;

### 18
I also will laugh at your disaster. I will mock when calamity overtakes you; when calamity overtakes you like a storm, when your disaster comes on like a whirlwind; when distress and anguish come on you.

### 19
Then will they call on me, but I will not answer. They will seek me diligently, but they will not find me; because they hated knowledge, and didn’t choose the fear of Yahweh.

### 20
They wanted none of my counsel. They despised all my reproof.

### 21
Therefore they will eat of the fruit of their own way, and be filled with their own schemes.

### 22
For the backsliding of the simple will kill them. The careless ease of fools will destroy them.

### 23
But whoever listens to me will dwell securely, and will be at ease, without fear of harm.”
