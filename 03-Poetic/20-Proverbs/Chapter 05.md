# Proverbs 5

### 1
My son, pay attention to my wisdom. Turn your ear to my understanding: that you may maintain discretion, that your lips may preserve knowledge.

### 2
For the lips of an adulteress drip honey. Her mouth is smoother than oil, but in the end she is as bitter as wormwood, and as sharp as a two-edged sword.

### 3
Her feet go down to death. Her steps lead straight to Sheol.

### 4
She gives no thought to the way of life. Her ways are crooked, and she doesn’t know it.

### 5
Now therefore, my sons, listen to me. Don’t depart from the words of my mouth.

### 6
Remove your way far from her. Don’t come near the door of her house, lest you give your honor to others, and your years to the cruel one;

### 7
lest strangers feast on your wealth, and your labors enrich another man’s house.

### 8
You will groan at your latter end, when your flesh and your body are consumed, and say, “How I have hated instruction, and my heart despised reproof;

### 9
neither have I obeyed the voice of my teachers, nor turned my ear to those who instructed me!

### 10
I have come to the brink of utter ruin, among the gathered assembly.”

### 11
Drink water out of your own cistern, running water out of your own well.

### 12
Should your springs overflow in the streets, streams of water in the public squares?

### 13
Let them be for yourself alone, not for strangers with you.

### 14
Let your spring be blessed. Rejoice in the wife of your youth.

### 15
A loving doe and a graceful deer— let her breasts satisfy you at all times. Be captivated always with her love.

### 16
For why should you, my son, be captivated with an adulteress? Why embrace the bosom of another?

### 17
For the ways of man are before Yahweh’s eyes. He examines all his paths.

### 18
The evil deeds of the wicked ensnare him. The cords of his sin hold him firmly.

### 19
He will die for lack of instruction. In the greatness of his folly, he will go astray.
