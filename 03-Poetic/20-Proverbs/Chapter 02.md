# Proverbs 2

### 1
My son, if you will receive my words, and store up my commandments within you; So as to turn your ear to wisdom, and apply your heart to understanding;

### 2
Yes, if you call out for discernment, and lift up your voice for understanding; If you seek her as silver, and search for her as for hidden treasures:

### 3
then you will understand the fear of Yahweh, and find the knowledge of God.

### 4
For Yahweh gives wisdom. Out of his mouth comes knowledge and understanding.

### 5
He lays up sound wisdom for the upright. He is a shield to those who walk in integrity; that he may guard the paths of justice, and preserve the way of his saints.

### 6
Then you will understand righteousness and justice, equity and every good path.

### 7
For wisdom will enter into your heart. Knowledge will be pleasant to your soul.

### 8
Discretion will watch over you. Understanding will keep you, to deliver you from the way of evil, from the men who speak perverse things;

### 9
who forsake the paths of uprightness, to walk in the ways of darkness; who rejoice to do evil, and delight in the perverseness of evil;

### 10
who are crooked in their ways, and wayward in their paths: To deliver you from the strange woman, even from the foreigner who flatters with her words;

### 11
who forsakes the friend of her youth, and forgets the covenant of her God: for her house leads down to death, her paths to the departed spirits.

### 12
None who go to her return again, neither do they attain to the paths of life: that you may walk in the way of good men, and keep the paths of the righteous.

### 13
For the upright will dwell in the land. The perfect will remain in it.

### 14
But the wicked will be cut off from the land. The treacherous will be rooted out of it.
