# Proverbs 31

### 1
The words of king Lemuel; the revelation which his mother taught him.

### 2
“Oh, my son! Oh, son of my womb! Oh, son of my vows!

### 3
Don’t give your strength to women, nor your ways to that which destroys kings.

### 4
It is not for kings, Lemuel; it is not for kings to drink wine; nor for princes to say, ‘Where is strong drink?’

### 5
lest they drink, and forget the law, and pervert the justice due to anyone who is afflicted.

### 6
Give strong drink to him who is ready to perish; and wine to the bitter in soul: Let him drink, and forget his poverty, and remember his misery no more.

### 7
Open your mouth for the mute, in the cause of all who are left desolate.

### 8
Open your mouth, judge righteously, and serve justice to the poor and needy.”

### 9
Who can find a worthy woman? For her price is far above rubies.

### 10
The heart of her husband trusts in her. He shall have no lack of gain.

### 11
She does him good, and not harm, all the days of her life.

### 12
She seeks wool and flax, and works eagerly with her hands.

### 13
She is like the merchant ships. She brings her bread from afar.

### 14
She rises also while it is yet night, gives food to her household, and portions for her servant girls.

### 15
She considers a field, and buys it. With the fruit of her hands, she plants a vineyard.

### 16
She arms her waist with strength, and makes her arms strong.

### 17
She perceives that her merchandise is profitable. Her lamp doesn’t go out by night.

### 18
She lays her hands to the distaff, and her hands hold the spindle.

### 19
She opens her arms to the poor; yes, she extends her hands to the needy.

### 20
She is not afraid of the snow for her household; for all her household are clothed with scarlet.

### 21
She makes for herself carpets of tapestry. Her clothing is fine linen and purple.

### 22
Her husband is respected in the gates, when he sits among the elders of the land.

### 23
She makes linen garments and sells them, and delivers sashes to the merchant.

### 24
Strength and dignity are her clothing. She laughs at the time to come.

### 25
She opens her mouth with wisdom. Faithful instruction is on her tongue.

### 26
She looks well to the ways of her household, and doesn’t eat the bread of idleness.

### 27
Her children rise up and call her blessed. Her husband also praises her: “Many women do noble things, but you excel them all.”

### 28
Charm is deceitful, and beauty is vain; but a woman who fears Yahweh, she shall be praised.

### 29
Give her of the fruit of her hands! Let her works praise her in the gates!
