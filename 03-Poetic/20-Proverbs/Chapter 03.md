# Proverbs 3

### 1
My son, don’t forget my teaching; but let your heart keep my commandments: for length of days, and years of life, and peace, will they add to you.

### 2
Don’t let kindness and truth forsake you. Bind them around your neck. Write them on the tablet of your heart.

### 3
So you will find favor, and good understanding in the sight of God and man.

### 4
Trust in Yahweh with all your heart, and don’t lean on your own understanding.

### 5
In all your ways acknowledge him, and he will make your paths straight.

### 6
Don’t be wise in your own eyes. Fear Yahweh, and depart from evil.

### 7
It will be health to your body, and nourishment to your bones.

### 8
Honor Yahweh with your substance, with the first fruits of all your increase: so your barns will be filled with plenty, and your vats will overflow with new wine.

### 9
My son, don’t despise Yahweh’s discipline, neither be weary of his reproof: for whom Yahweh loves, he reproves; even as a father reproves the son in whom he delights.

### 10
Happy is the man who finds wisdom, the man who gets understanding.

### 11
For her good profit is better than getting silver, and her return is better than fine gold.

### 12
She is more precious than rubies. None of the things you can desire are to be compared to her.

### 13
Length of days is in her right hand. In her left hand are riches and honor.

### 14
Her ways are ways of pleasantness. All her paths are peace.

### 15
She is a tree of life to those who lay hold of her. Happy is everyone who retains her.

### 16
By wisdom Yahweh founded the earth. By understanding, he established the heavens.

### 17
By his knowledge, the depths were broken up, and the skies drop down the dew.

### 18
My son, let them not depart from your eyes. Keep sound wisdom and discretion: so they will be life to your soul, and grace for your neck.

### 19
Then you shall walk in your way securely. Your foot won’t stumble.

### 20
When you lie down, you will not be afraid. Yes, you will lie down, and your sleep will be sweet.

### 21
Don’t be afraid of sudden fear, neither of the desolation of the wicked, when it comes: for Yahweh will be your confidence, and will keep your foot from being taken.

### 22
Don’t withhold good from those to whom it is due, when it is in the power of your hand to do it.

### 23
Don’t say to your neighbor, “Go, and come again; tomorrow I will give it to you,” when you have it by you.

### 24
Don’t devise evil against your neighbor, since he dwells securely by you.

### 25
Don’t strive with a man without cause, if he has done you no harm.

### 26
Don’t envy the man of violence. Choose none of his ways.

### 27
For the perverse is an abomination to Yahweh, but his friendship is with the upright.

### 28
Yahweh’s curse is in the house of the wicked, but he blesses the habitation of the righteous.

### 29
Surely he mocks the mockers, but he gives grace to the humble.

### 30
The wise will inherit glory, but shame will be the promotion of fools.
